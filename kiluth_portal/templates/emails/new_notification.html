{#- Shadows frappe/templates/emails/new_notification.html: the email a Notification Log sends (frappe.desk.doctype.notification_log.send_notification_email). The Portal's most frequent email: "X assigned a new task Task TASK-0001 to you", "Your assignment on ... has been removed by X", mentions, shares. Frappe passes only: body_content (the log's subject, HTML with / around names), description (the log's email_content: the task description or the comment, markdown/HTML, may be empty), doc_link, and document_type + document_name when the log points at a document.
